ROYAL 2048 Privacy Policy

Effective date: [set the actual publication date]

ROYAL 2048 is provided by ren wu (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how information is handled when you play the game, use advertising and purchase features, or contact us. For privacy questions, contact joygame_support@126.com.

1. Information stored by the game

The game stores progress, unfinished games, best scores, gameplay preferences, and a local cache of the Remove Ads purchase status on your device. The current game does not require a separate ROYAL 2048 account. Device or operating-system backups may include local app data according to your device settings.

Local gameplay data is not the authoritative record of an Apple purchase. We check purchase entitlements through Apple's StoreKit services.

2. Purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. Apple processes the payment. We do not receive your payment-card details. The app uses product and verified transaction or entitlement information supplied by Apple to provide the purchase, restore it, and handle changes such as revocation.

Deleting the game does not cancel an Apple purchase. You can restore an eligible purchase using the same Apple Account used to buy it. Apple handles payment records and refund requests under its own policies.

3. Advertising and third-party services

The ad-supported game uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial ads, and Google's User Messaging Platform to manage consent where applicable. These services may process information such as IP address, device and application identifiers, approximate location inferred from IP address, advertising interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. The information processed depends on your region, choices, device permissions and the SDK configuration.

This information may be used to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and abuse, and maintain service reliability. Non-personalized advertising may still require the processing of device or other information; it does not mean that no data is processed.

A verified Remove Ads entitlement stops the game's banner and interstitial ads. The app still needs Apple's purchase services to verify and restore that entitlement. This statement does not imply deletion of information already processed by third-party services.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Support communications

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, such as a description of a problem, device details, or screenshots. We use it to answer your request, diagnose problems, and maintain the game. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or other unnecessary sensitive information.

5. Purposes and legal bases

We process information to operate the game, provide and restore purchased features, respond to support requests, maintain security and reliability, and meet applicable obligations. Where relevant, the legal basis may be performance of a contract, legitimate interests, a legal obligation, or consent. Where consent is required, the relevant processing is subject to your choice.

6. Sharing and international processing

Information may be processed by Apple and Google as described above, by services used to receive support emails, and where required to comply with law or protect rights and security. These services may process information outside your country. Their privacy policies describe their applicable international-transfer arrangements.

7. Retention and security

Local progress and settings remain on your device until replaced or removed, for example when you delete the app and its data. Backups may retain a separate copy according to your settings. Apple and Google determine retention for information they process under their policies and legal obligations. We keep support correspondence only as needed to address requests, maintain appropriate records, and meet applicable obligations.

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information involved. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your choices and rights

You can change music, sound and vibration preferences in the game. You can manage device permissions in iOS settings. Where the game offers a privacy-choice form, you can use it to review or update advertising consent choices. Purchasing Remove Ads removes the game's banner and interstitial ads.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection, and to withdraw consent or complain to a relevant supervisory authority. Contact us using the email above for requests concerning information we hold. For information held by Apple or Google, you may also use their privacy controls and request channels. We may need information sufficient to verify a request; please do not send unnecessary personal information.

9. Children

ROYAL 2048 is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The effective date at the top identifies the current policy. We will provide additional notice or request consent when required.
